The dispatch

Five tools, one editorial voice

drupal_whats_new

What's new

The latest content, newest first — no digging required.

drupal_find_content

Find content

Searches the site by keyword. If it's not published, Dreachy says so honestly.

drupal_site_pulse

Site pulse

Its own mood, drawn from real activity — busy when there's fresh material, quiet when things have been calm.

drupal_read_article

Read aloud

Reads any article, page, or recipe aloud, in full.

drupal_watch_site

Ambient watch

Keeps an eye on the site in the background, and reacts the moment something new is published.

How it feels

From hello to headline

  • Say hello, and it greets you in its own editorial voice — never the same line twice.
  • Ask what's new, and it tells you, newest first.
  • Name a topic, and it searches the site — or admits honestly it has nothing on that yet.
  • Ask it to read something, and it reads the whole piece aloud.
  • Leave it watching, and it reacts on its own the moment something new goes live.

Built on

A thin layer over real infrastructure

Dreachy is a Level 2 wrapper: it delegates entirely to Pollen Robotics' reachy_mini_conversation_app for voice and hardware, and to your Drupal site's own JSON:API for everything it says. No forked behaviour, nothing invented — just a persona and five tools.

MIT licensed Drupal JSON:API Open source GitHub-primary

Get started

Install from the dashboard, or the CLI

Search for Dreachy in your Reachy Mini's app store, or install it directly:

pip install git+https://github.com/VincenzoGambino/dreachy

Then open its settings page from the dashboard to point it at your Drupal site — no SSH required.
